## Skipped subjects

- **Coffee pot** — no clearly-PD period illustration found on Commons (only modern photos or off-theme museum silverware).
- **Tombstone / boot hill** — only modern photographs found; no PD 19th-century illustration.
- **Whiskey bottle** — the only clearly-PD period bottle ads found were unsuitable (1882 'Belle of Nelson' ad contains nudity); replaced with a tasteful 1912 newspaper whiskey ad (whiskey-ad.png).
- **Playing cards (object)** — covered by saloon-cards.jpg (faro game photo) instead of a card-face scan.
- **Calm prairie landscape** — best PD prairie scenes found were dramatic (prairie-fire.jpg) or panoramic (buffalo-river-crossing.jpg); both included.
- **Gold coins** — covered by gold-panning.jpg.
- Bewick's 1784 wolf woodcut skipped: its Commons page is tagged CC BY-SA rather than PD; NIE 1905 wolf/coyote plate used instead.
